Digital Delivery Policy

How and when what you bought reaches you, and exactly what to do if it does not.

Last updated October 27, 2026.

Everything here is digital

Thistledown Kitchen does not ship anything physical. There are no parcels, no postage charges, no tracking numbers and no delivery addresses. Everything we sell is delivered electronically to the email address you give at checkout.

A download link is emailed within 15 minutes of payment, and the same link stays valid for one year.

The email you should expect

The delivery email comes from our own domain, thistledownkitchen.com. It contains a direct download link. It never contains an attachment, and it never asks you to log in, create an account, enter a password or confirm payment details. If you receive a message claiming to be from us that asks for any of those things, it is not from us — please forward it to hello@thistledownkitchen.com and delete it.

How long your link lasts

Download links stay valid for one year from purchase and can be used as many times as you like within that year, on as many of your own devices as you like. Download the file and keep your own copy; that copy is yours permanently and does not expire when the link does.

If your link has expired and you want the file again, write to us. We will reissue it, free, with no time limit on asking. We keep order records for seven years, so we can almost always find a purchase from years ago.

If nothing arrives

In order, because this resolves it nine times out of ten:

  1. Wait ten minutes. Delivery is usually immediate but mail systems occasionally queue.
  2. Check the spam, junk and promotions folders. This is by far the most common cause. Automated mail with a download link in it is exactly what a spam filter is built to be suspicious of.
  3. Search your mailbox for "thistledownkitchen.com" rather than scrolling. Filters sometimes file a message somewhere unexpected.
  4. Check for a typo in the address you entered. One wrong character and the message is somewhere else entirely.
  5. Write to us at hello@thistledownkitchen.com with the address you used at checkout and roughly when you bought. We resend by hand, to any address you like, the same business day.

You can also telephone (717) 555‑0142 during office hours: Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm Eastern Time.

Making sure our mail reaches you

Adding hello@thistledownkitchen.com to your contacts before you buy makes delivery problems much less likely. Work and school addresses are the most likely to block automated mail with links in it; a personal address is usually the safer choice at checkout.

If the file will not open

Everything we sell is PDF, which opens on every current version of Windows, macOS, iOS, iPadOS, Android and Linux without extra software. If a file will not open, it is almost always a partial download. Download it again on a stable connection. If it still fails, write to us and we will send the file a different way.

Work and school addresses

Corporate and institutional mail servers are the single most common reason a delivery fails. Many of them quarantine any automated message containing a download link, and they frequently do it silently — the message does not bounce, it simply never appears, and there is no spam folder for you to find it in. A personal address is the safer choice at checkout. If you have already used a work address and nothing has arrived, write to us from any other address and we will send it there instead.

Changing the delivery address after you have paid

Just ask. There is no process and no charge. Email us from either the address you used or the one you would prefer, tell us which purchase it relates to, and we will reissue the link to the new address the same business day. Because there are no accounts on this site, there is nothing for you to log into and update — we simply resend it.

What we never send

We will never email you asking for a password, a card number, a bank detail or a confirmation of payment, and we will never send you an executable file or an attachment of any kind. Our delivery messages contain a link and nothing else. If you receive anything claiming to be from Thistledown Kitchen that asks for those things, it is not from us: forward it to hello@thistledownkitchen.com and then delete it.

Problems that are not delivery problems

If the file arrived and opened but is not what you wanted, that is a refund question rather than a delivery one. You have 14 days: see the Refund and Cancellation Policy.
