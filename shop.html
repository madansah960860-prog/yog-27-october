How payment works

Payment is handled by an external payment provider on their own secure page. We never see or store your card number. You will be told the exact amount before you confirm, and the charge on your statement will show the name of the payment provider alongside ours.

After payment you are returned to a page on this site confirming the order, and the download email follows within 15 minutes. If it has not arrived within an hour, check the spam folder first and then write to hello@thistledownkitchen.com with the email address you used. We will resend it by hand the same business day.

Printing the files well

Everything is laid out for US Letter paper with margins wide enough that nothing is lost to a printer's unprintable edge. Print at 100 percent rather than "fit to page"; scaling shrinks the type and, on the card sets, throws the cut marks out of alignment so the fronts and backs no longer line up.

For the recipe cards, print double-sided with the short edge flipped. If your printer only does single-sided, print the odd pages, put the stack back in face down, and print the even pages. The card sets are ordered so that works. Card stock up to 65 lb feeds through most home printers and makes a card that survives a wet counter; plain paper works perfectly well too and you can always put it in a sleeve.
